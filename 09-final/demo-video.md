## Section 1: Hook, 0-10 Seconds

**Script:** "Your class starts soon, you are running to the bus stop, the tablo says the bus will arrive in 35 minutes, but don't they travel every 30 minutes?."

**Shot:** At a Bus #3 stop near KIU or Colchis Fountain. One team member checks the official countdown or looks at the stop display, then looks frustrated.

**On camera:** Nikoloz Kvirikashili or Giorgi Mikaberidze.

## Section 2: Problem, 10-25 Seconds

**Script:** "KIU commuters told us they lose 5 to 12 hours every week leaving early because the official tracker resets, freezes, or shows buses that never arrive. They either lose time by waiting, or lose money by calling taxi."

**Shot:** Quick cuts: phone showing a countdown, student waiting, student checking time, student walking away or opening a taxi app.

**On camera:** Two team members, one filming and one acting as commuter.

## Section 3: Product, 25-45 Seconds

**Script:** "Bandersnatch gives students an honest pre-departure check. Pick your stop, check Bus #3,see the live status and confirm you are on the bus, you can even see the live map of the bus routes."

**Shot:** Real product on a phone in portrait orientation. Open the deployed app, select a stop, check status, then show the live map or feedback/reporting surface.

**Device:** Phone, portrait, held by the Tech Lead.

**Exact flow:**

1. Open https://product-capstone-2026.vercel.app/en.
2. Select a Bus #3 stop and destination/direction.
3. Show the status result and one trust/community signal.

## Section 4: Proof, 45-55 Seconds

**Script:** "In rapid testing, 15 of 20 observed users checked bus status, and 12 completed boarding after checking."

**Shot:** Quick laptop shot or overlaid text with the metric and source: `03-build/experiments/experiment-results.md`.

**Metric:** 15 of 20 core status checks; 12 of 15 boardings; May 19-21, 2026 rapid test.

## Section 5: Call to Action, 55-60 Seconds

**Script:** "Try it now: product-capstone-2026 dot vercel dot app slash en."

**Shot:** Team member holds the phone with the live app and URL visible.

**QR code prepared:** No. URL will be shown and spoken clearly.
