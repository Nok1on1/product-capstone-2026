**BANDERSNATCH**  
Honest Bus #3 status before students leave for the stop.  
https://product-capstone-2026.vercel.app/en

## Problem

KIU off-campus students relying on Bus #3 lose 5-12 hours weekly because official tracking tools show misleading arrival times. Interviews show 1.5-3 hour buffers, 6-10 GEL emergency taxi spend, missed quizzes, and sleep disruption.

## Solution

Bandersnatch gives Bus #3 commuters a mobile-first pre-departure status check with stop selection, CONFIRMED/ESTIMATED context, crowding reports, and peer-powered route visibility.
