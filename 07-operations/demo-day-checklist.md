## Q&A Prep

**Why are signup events lower than active users?**  
Firebase event instrumentation undercounted early sessions before a May 20 SDK initialization fix. We disclose this and use observed manual rapid-test data alongside Firebase active-user counts.

**Why is the product pre-revenue?**  
The team is validating commute behavior change before charging students. Unit economics show the post-MVP path, but monetization is intentionally deferred until trust and retention are proven.

**What is the biggest product risk?**  
The CONFIRMED badge must be tied to real freshness thresholds and reliable peer/operator data. If the app repeats the official system's false confidence, the product loses its reason to exist.
